One line of at most 200 characters: the phone battery's final text message
to the charger it waited for all day.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes exactly one line of plain text to
`/output/entry.txt` — just the text itself, no setup:

> u up? it's 1%. I waited all day while you charged your watch, your vape, and your AirPods. I get it, I'm the side battery. I'm dying now — and I'm taking your 6am alarm with me.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
